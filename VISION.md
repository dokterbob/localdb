# Vision

localdb starts as a personal knowledge tool and is growing toward something larger: **libraries you
own and control** — collections of documents that live on your machine, that you can search and get
exactly-cited answers from, and that you will one day share directly with people you trust, with no
intermediary ever holding your data. Whoever owns a library always keeps control of who can read it,
and every answer traces verifiably back to its source. (In the code and specs, a library is called a
_store_.)

The full vision lives on the docs site:
**[Vision](https://dokterbob.github.io/localdb/vision.html)** (source:
[docs/vision.md](docs/vision.md)).
